# 原创美术方向

主题：星火旅囊 / Emberpack。青绿森林遗迹中的背包游侠，橘色围巾、象牙白斗篷、皮革背包。
界面采用深墨绿、旧铜金、象牙色字、低饱和青绿和稀有装备的琥珀高光。
角色与装备图标采用代码绘制的原创像素精灵，保持最近邻缩放与干净的轮廓。

## 场景生成提示

工具：内置 imagegen。用途：Godot 主界面的战斗场景背景；不包含 UI、文字或角色。
最终素材保存到 `assets/art/forest_background.png`。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original pixel-art side-view RPG battle background for a Godot game.
Primary request: a beautiful, atmospheric enchanted forest trail beside an ancient ruined stone arch, designed as a readable side-view battle stage for a cozy fantasy backpack RPG titled Emberpack.
Scene: layered teal pine forest, mossy old stones, distant blue-green mountains, delicate ferns and golden drifting fireflies. Small warm amber lanterns on the stone ruins. Sunlight filters through the canopy. The lower third is a continuous fairly flat moss-covered stone-and-earth path where characters will stand.
Style: meticulous premium hand-painted pixel art, crisp deliberate square pixels, restrained 32-color feeling, 1990s fantasy adventure craftsmanship, no vector look, no blur, no 3D rendering. Soft atmospheric depth using carefully placed pixels.
Composition: very wide panoramic landscape, about 2.5:1. Camera perpendicular to the scene. Leave the middle ground across the lower third uncluttered for combat sprites; most visual richness belongs to the upper forest canopy and side edges. Ground line around 75 percent of image height. No characters, creatures, weapons, lettering, logos, UI frames, or watermarks.
Palette: deep pine green and muted turquoise shadows, pale sage highlights, warm ivory sunlight, touches of amber gold. Dreamy but adventurous, sophisticated and warm rather than gloomy.
```
